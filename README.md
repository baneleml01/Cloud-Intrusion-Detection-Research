# Cloud Intrusion Detection Research

## Overview
This research project investigates best practices for intrusion detection and threat mitigation in cloud computing environments. It focuses on the challenges of securing dynamic cloud systems and evaluates modern detection and prevention techniques.

---

## Research Problem
Traditional security approaches struggle to adapt to the dynamic nature of cloud environments, increasing the risk of cyberattacks, unauthorised access, and insufficient intrusion detection capabilities.

---

## Research Objectives

### Primary Objective:
To develop best practices for enhancing intrusion detection and threat mitigation strategies in cloud environments.

### Secondary Objectives:

**SO1 (Literature Review):**  
To identify current challenges and limitations of intrusion detection systems (IDS) in cloud environments.

**SO2 (Systematic Literature Review):**  
To identify effective intrusion detection systems and automated threat response mechanisms implemented in cloud environments.

**SO3 (Semi-Structured Interviews):**  
To obtain cybersecurity professionals’ perspectives and recommendations on intrusion detection systems and response mechanisms in cloud environments.

---

## Research Methodology

This study uses a qualitative research approach to achieve the research objectives. Critical reasoning is applied to synthesise findings across the different methods used in the study.

The research employs a multi-method approach consisting of:
- Narrative Literature Review to identify existing challenges
- Systematic Literature Review to identify effective practices
- Semi-Structured Interviews to gather expert perspectives and recommendations

This approach supports methodological triangulation, which enhances the validity and reliability of the findings. The integration of insights from literature, analysis, and professional input enables the development of well-informed best practices for intrusion detection and threat mitigation in cloud environments.
